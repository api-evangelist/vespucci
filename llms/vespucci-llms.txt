# Vespucci

> Vespucci (Vespucci Analytics) is a product-analytics platform that helps teams understand why their users engage with a product. Instead of replacing existing analytics, it connects to the event data a company already collects in Amplitude or Segment and applies statistical and AI models to automatically surface the drivers of activation, retention, churn, and engagement, with 24/7 monitoring, automated alerts, user segmentation, and story-style dashboards. Founded in 2021 in Brussels, Belgium; backed by Seedcamp.

## Product

- [Vespucci — Understand your users like never before](https://vespuccianalytics.com/): marketing site and product overview.
- Connects to existing analytics (Amplitude, Segment, Reverse ETL); no new tagging plan required.
- AI/statistical models explain activation, retention, churn, and engagement; 24/7 monitoring with change alerts.
- Segments can be pushed to activation tools including OneSignal, MailChimp, and Brevo.
